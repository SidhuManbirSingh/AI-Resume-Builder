### Financial Market Data Extraction & Standardization Tool | [GitHub](https://github.com/SidhuManbirSingh/Stock_Market)
*Python | Pandas | Financial Data Pipelines | REST APIs | ETL*

- Engineered an automated financial data extraction and normalization engine in Python to ingest multi-source equities and market time-series metrics.
- Developed cleaning and preprocessing routines leveraging Pandas and NumPy to handle missing records, normalize timestamps, and standardize ticker fundamentals.
- Structured high-throughput ETL workflows exporting sanitized market metrics into structured JSON and CSV formats for downstream modeling.
- Implemented robust error handling, API rate limiting backoff strategies, and modular pipeline design for reliable data collection.
