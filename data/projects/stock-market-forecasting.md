### Financial Market Data Pipeline & Hybrid Forecasting
*Python | Pandas | NumPy | yfinance | LSTM/GRU | Financial Modeling*

- Built an automated data pipeline fetching historical price actions, dividend distributions, and quarterly financial statements via Yahoo Finance and Quandl APIs.
- Processed over 12 years of daily market records, computing rolling volatility, percentage returns, and dividend yield histories.
- Standardized complex multi-company financial data into uniform scales (billions) and derived critical balance sheet ratios (Debt-to-Equity, ROA, EBITDA margin).
- Developed a hybrid LSTM-GRU deep learning architecture to capture sequential long-term dependencies in financial time-series data.
- Exported consolidated, clean datasets and comparative metrics across multiple equity tickers for algorithmic analysis.
