- Modeled commercial property Preventative Maintenance, Technician Dispatch, and Service Contract Coordination workflows, identifying operational bottlenecks and redesigning dispatch protocols to reduce manual steps by ~40%.
- Designed governance artifacts including Environmental & Risk Management Inspection checklists, Life Safety Systems testing protocols, and Leasehold Improvements guidelines.
- Facilitated operational walkthroughs with team members to standardize dispatch handoffs, vendor coordination, and KPI tracking (response SLA adherence, time-saved).
