### Preventative Maintenance & BMS Compliance Tracking
- Designed automated compliance tracking logs and diagnostic tools for Computerized Control Systems (BMS/BAS) to track preventative maintenance schedules and sensor metrics across HVAC units, chillers, pumps, boilers, and air handlers.
- Streamlined incident logging, cost-replacement analysis, and work order tracking across enterprise facility management platforms (MRI Angus / WPM Plus workflows), improving response times and technical dispatch efficiency.
- Developed standardized maintenance logs and Job Hazard Assessments (JHA) in full compliance with OH&S guidelines and enterprise commercial property standards.
